Privacy Policy for Neon Jumper – Jumping Home

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Developer: PatDattelt

Current version: This policy covers the Android game, its accompanying website and Google AdMob advertising planned for a later release. Real advertising is disabled in the current internal Google Play test build (version 0.1.8). Separate debug builds may show official Google demo advertisements to adult testers. Real ads are intended to be enabled for adults only after the relevant privacy, consent and release checks have succeeded. Until that happens, the production advertising processing described below does not take place.

This policy applies to the Android game “Neon Jumper – Jumping Home” and its accompanying information website. Google's own privacy notices additionally apply to data processed by the Google Play Store.

2. Age screening and protection of minors

The game is intended for children, teenagers and adults. Before playing, users are asked for their date of birth to determine an age category (under 13, 13–17 or 18 and over). The full date of birth is not retained after the age group has been determined and is not sent to a server operated by the developer. Only the resulting age category is stored locally on the device. Users can repeat the age check in the game settings.

The age category controls advertising safeguards. The game logic does not initiate Google AdMob ad requests for users under 18 or with an unknown age category. Players do not need to create an account. Visiting an external website through a link may cause the website's hosting provider to process technically necessary connection data (see section 8).

3. Locally stored game data and backups

The game stores information such as completed and unlocked levels, scores and stars, coins and orbs, unlocked or obtained game items, character skins, language, music, sound and control preferences and the local age category. In the Android app this data is stored locally using WebView storage. The developer does not operate a player-account system or a separate game-save cloud database for these records.

Local storage is necessary to provide game progress and settings. The game does not automatically send those records to PatDattelt. Progress can be reset in the game, deleted through Android app-data settings or removed by uninstalling the app. Players may export and import local backup files themselves. Exported backups remain at the location selected by the player until they are deleted there; the app does not automatically upload them to the developer.

4. Advertising through Google AdMob

The game is intended to be funded by advertisements only; it has no in-app purchases with real money. Once live advertising is enabled, adult users may see occasional full-screen ads and may choose to watch rewarded advertisements to receive in-game rewards. Ad requests require a confirmed age category of 18 or over and a positive result from Google's consent-management checks. Whether ads are personalised depends on applicable settings and privacy choices.

The Android app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, when the SDK is used to request advertisements it may collect and transmit the following categories of personal or device-related information:

  • IP address, which may be used to derive an approximate location;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as startup time, errors, responsiveness and battery use;
  • app, device and, where applicable, other technically available identifiers.

The data may be used and shared by Google and participating advertising technology partners for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. The permission for the Android advertising ID (AD_ID) is explicitly removed from the current app manifest. This does not exclude the processing of other technical identifiers. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted data in transit using TLS.

Details about the data recipients, retention periods and possible international transfers can be found in Google's Privacy Policy and in provider information linked from any consent form displayed. PatDattelt has limited control over retention periods for data that Google processes independently.

5. Consent and privacy settings

For adult users, the Android app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent status and, where required under applicable rules and made available by Google, present privacy notices and choices. The relevant choice is taken into account for advertising. Where consent is legally required, the corresponding processing is based on Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Consent can be withdrawn with effect for the future. Other legal grounds for strictly necessary security processing may include Article 6(1)(f) GDPR where its requirements are met.

If Google makes a privacy options form available, it can be opened in the game under Settings → Privacy → Manage advertising consent. The game requests ads only when UMP reports canRequestAds() as true. This technical result does not necessarily mean a user has explicitly consented: in some cases Google determines that an additional consent form is not required. If ads cannot be requested, the game does not show an AdMob ad. The game logic does not request ads for minors in any case.

Current testing status: The EU consent flow for future live advertising has not yet been fully verified. Live advertising remains disabled in the current Google Play test build and will only be enabled after additional checks of the SDK and required disclosures.

6. Retention and security

Local progress is generally kept on the device until users reset it, clear the app's data or remove the app. Separately exported backups remain wherever the user saved them. Technical ad and consent data may be subject to the retention practices of the relevant service providers. Google states that its SDK uses encrypted connections in transit. This does not mean that a backup file manually exported by a user is automatically encrypted.

7. Contact by email

If you contact PatDattelt, the information you send is used to handle and respond to your inquiry. Depending on the nature of the request, processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. Messages are kept only as long as necessary to handle the inquiry and comply with applicable retention obligations or protect legitimate claims. Please do not send unnecessary personal information, especially sensitive data concerning children.

8. Information website (GitHub Pages)

The accompanying information website is served through GitHub Pages. When visitors open it, their IP address, browser and connection details and request timestamps are necessarily transmitted to the hosting infrastructure and may be logged. This serves the legitimate interest in providing a secure, reliable website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website does not use developer-added visitor analytics, advertising trackers or external web fonts.

For details on GitHub's data handling and international transfers, see GitHub's Privacy Statement. The website operator has limited control over GitHub's technical log retention.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent for the future. You may also lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection supervisory authority. Contact neon-nomad@gmx.de to exercise your rights. You can delete locally stored game data yourself through the app or Android settings; for data processed independently by Google, Google's own privacy and account tools may also be relevant.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the game features, integrated SDKs or personal-data processing change. Before enabling real AdMob ads, we will specifically recheck the actual data flows, consent options and Google Play data declarations. The current version published on this page applies.
